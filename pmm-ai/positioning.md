**Invoke:** `/pmm-ai positioning [vs-legacy-cmms | standalone-agent | platform-differentiator | cdo-pitch | roi-first]`

---

## Methodology

Build Atom positioning using April Dunford's "Obviously Awesome" framework with one critical Atom-specific addition:

**The "Live vs. Roadmap" Distinction — The Spine of All Atom Positioning**
Every competitor claims AI. The positioning that wins is not "our AI is better" — it's "our AI is in production. Theirs is on a roadmap."
This distinction must appear, adapted for context, in every positioning output:
- "Berkeley UAE: 80% autonomous resolution. Live. Not a pilot."
- "Charter Hall: 619 invoice errors caught. In 4 months. Not a beta."
The live vs. roadmap distinction is what converts skeptical AI buyers.

**April Dunford 5-Component Positioning (AI-specific application):**
1. Competitive alternatives: What would they do if Atom didn't exist? (Maximo AI overlay / generic LLM wrapper / manual operations / RPA)
2. Unique attributes: What does Atom have that alternatives genuinely don't? (FM-native execution, not bolt-on; standalone deployable; CMMS-agnostic; live proof)
3. Value: What do those attributes enable? (Autonomous FM operations, not just AI insights)
4. Target customers: Who cares most? (CDOs needing production proof · FMSPs needing helpdesk scale · REITs needing invoice accuracy)
5. Market frame: What category does Atom define? (Agentic FM Operations — not "AI for FM")

**"Only Facilio Atom" Test:**
Every positioning claim must pass: "Only Facilio Atom [claim] because [unique attribute] proven by [live evidence]."
If you can't complete that sentence — it's a brand assertion, not positioning.

---

## Execution Rules

- The status quo we're displacing is not just "legacy CMMS" — it's "AI that recommends but doesn't act"
- Never position Atom as "AI features added to Facilio CMMS" — position it as a standalone AI execution layer that happens to integrate with any CMMS
- The false fix must name what's wrong with competitor AI specifically: bolt-on architecture, SI-dependent, reporting-only, no FM workflow execution
- Proof must be specific: agent name + customer name + metric + timeframe. Not "customers have seen great results."
- What Not to Say section is mandatory — it's what teams default to when they haven't internalized the positioning

## Angle-Specific Guidance

### vs-legacy-cmms
**Category:** Agentic FM Operations — the layer that turns your CMMS from a system of record into a system of action
**Core reframe:** "Your CMMS captures what happened. Atom acts on what's happening."
**False fix to name:** Maximo's AI overlay — generates reports and recommendations. A human still has to read the report, create the work order, and dispatch the technician.
**Only Atom statement:** "Only Facilio Atom autonomously closes the loop from alert → intake → dispatch → resolution — without a human at each step. Proven: Berkeley UAE, 80% autonomous in 30 days."

### standalone-agent
**Category:** FM-native AI agent — deploys on top of any CMMS, no rip-and-replace
**Core reframe:** "You don't need to replace your CMMS to get AI execution. Atom deploys on top of what you have in 2–6 weeks."
**Target:** Accounts with Maximo, FSI, or any legacy CMMS who assume AI requires a platform switch
**Only Atom statement:** "Only Facilio Atom deploys as a standalone AI execution layer on top of any existing CMMS — including Maximo. No SI. No rip-and-replace. Live in 2–6 weeks."

### platform-differentiator
**Category:** The AI execution layer that makes Facilio CMMS a system of action
**Core reframe:** "Every CMMS vendor is adding AI features. Atom isn't a feature — it's the execution layer that runs FM workflows end-to-end."
**Target:** Accounts evaluating Facilio CMMS who haven't heard the Atom story yet
**Only Atom statement:** "Only Facilio has AI agents native to the FM workflow engine — not bolted on. Mira, Luca, FM Copilot, and OpsVision don't sit on top of the CMMS. They run inside it."

### cdo-pitch
**Category:** Production AI for FM — measurable ROI in 30–90 days, not a 6-month POC
**Core reframe:** "The reason your AI POCs don't scale isn't the technology. It's that the technology was built to advise, not to execute."
**Target:** CDOs burned by AI pilots who need to show board-level ROI
**Only Atom statement:** "Only Facilio Atom has live production proof at enterprise scale — 276 calls at Berkeley UAE in 30 days, 619 invoice errors caught at Charter Hall in 4 months. Not beta customers. Production."

### roi-first
**Category:** FM cost reduction through autonomous execution — the fastest ROI path in FM tech
**Core reframe:** "Charter Hall paid $25K/year for Luca. In 4 months, it caught 619 errors and eliminated 70+ FM hours per month. The ROI was visible by month 2."
**Target:** CFOs and economic buyers who need a financial justification before technical evaluation
**Only Atom statement:** "Only Facilio Atom delivers measurable, quantifiable ROI within a single quarter — not at contract renewal. Charter Hall's numbers prove it."
