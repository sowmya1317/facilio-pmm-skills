# /pmm-ai content — AI Content Brief Builder
**Invoke:** `/pmm-ai content [topic]`

---

## Methodology

Build AI-specific content briefs using the same base framework as `/pmm content` (Skyscraper + Crestodina belief test + B2B Institute research), with three Atom-specific rules:

**1. FM-Grounded, Not AI-Trend**
Every piece must be anchored in FM operational reality — not AI industry trends. The insight must be something an FM Director, CDO, or COO would recognize from their own operations.
- RIGHT: "62% of FM work happens outside the CMMS. Atom closes that gap."
- WRONG: "AI is revolutionizing the facilities management industry."

**2. Proof-First Rule**
Every key argument must be anchored in Berkeley UAE or Charter Hall data — or a verified FM industry stat. No unanchored claims about what AI "can" do. Show what it does, with proof.

**3. Live vs. Roadmap Distinction in Every Piece**
Every Atom content piece must make clear: Facilio Atom is in production. This is not a "future of FM" piece — this is a "what's happening right now at Berkeley and Charter Hall" piece. The distinction builds credibility with AI-skeptical buyers.

**What NOT to include (mandatory section):**
- Generic AI trend language ("AI is transforming FM")
- Roadmap claims presented as current capabilities
- Competitor attacks — reframe through category definition
- Any claim that can't be backed by Berkeley, Charter Hall, or a verified industry stat

---

## Execution Rules

- Belief change statement is mandatory — if reading this piece changes nothing the reader believes, the angle is wrong
- Title options must include at least one that names Berkeley UAE or Charter Hall explicitly — proof-led titles convert better than generic AI titles for this audience
- Facilio Atom enters the narrative at minimum one-third of the way through — earn the right to mention the product
- Agent-level specificity preferred: name Mira or Luca rather than "Facilio's AI" wherever possible
- Check AI GTM asset library in pmm-ai/skills.md before building — surface existing assets first

## Content-Type Specific Guidance

### Blog / Thought Leadership (Awareness)
**Best angles for Atom content:**
- "Why FM AI POCs fail — and what Berkeley UAE did differently" (CDO/CAIO audience)
- "80% autonomous in 30 days: what that actually means for FM helpdesk operations" (FM Director audience)
- "The invoice error your CMMS will never catch" (CFO/Finance audience)
- "Your CMMS records. Atom executes. The difference is $619 errors." (CRE/REIT audience)

**Structure:** Hook (FM operational reality) → Problem (why manual is breaking) → Live proof (Berkeley or Charter Hall) → How Atom works (agent-specific, 2 paragraphs max) → CTA (case study or demo)

**Length:** 800–1,200 words. Enough to make the argument. Not a product tour.

### Ebook / Whitepaper (Consideration)
**Best angles:**
- "The Autonomous FM Playbook: How to go from AI pilot to production in 30 days"
- "AI in FM: What's live, what's on a roadmap, and how to tell the difference"
- "The FM Director's Guide to Evaluating AI Agents"

**Structure:** Market context (AI pressure on FM, board mandates) → The POC failure pattern → What live production looks like (Berkeley + Charter Hall as the case studies) → How to evaluate AI agents (3 questions: native/bolt-on? production/roadmap? executes/recommends?) → Atom overview → CTA

**Proof requirement:** Both Berkeley UAE and Charter Hall must be featured. Specific numbers, specific timelines.

### LinkedIn Article (Awareness)
**Hook rule:** Must work as a standalone LinkedIn post. If you removed everything after the first 3 lines, would the hook still earn a reaction? If no — rewrite.
**Structure:** Hook (FM operational truth) → 3 short paragraphs → One proof stat → One question to drive comments
**Length:** 600–800 words max
**Atom mention:** Once, naturally, with agent name and proof stat. Never a hard sell.

### Case Study Format (Decision)
**Agent-specific structure:**
- Mira case study: [Customer] + helpdesk volume + pain → Mira deployment (UAT timeline) → autonomous resolution rate → what the team does now
- Luca case study: [Customer] + invoice volume + error rate → Luca deployment → detection rate progression → hours saved

**Proof requirement:** Every stat verbatim from the verified library. No rounding up. No inflation.

### Video Script (Awareness / Consideration)
**PAS for Atom:** Problem (the 2am call no one answers) → Agitate (what that costs in SLA breaches, tenant satisfaction, team burnout) → Solution (Mira handles it — Berkeley proof)
**Length:** 90 seconds max. 180–225 words.
**Opening:** Never start with "Introducing Facilio Atom." Start with the operational problem.
**Proof on screen:** Berkeley or Charter Hall stat must appear visually — not just in VO.

### IFM ABM Content (All Stages)
Match content angle to the 7 IFM ABM themes from pmm-ai/skills.md:
- Themes 1+2 (not winning clients, losing RFPs): Awareness — "The FMSP that automated its helpdesk and won 3x more contracts"
- Themes 3+4 (opportunity cost, selling outcomes not services): Consideration — "From service provider to strategic partner: what AI makes possible"
- Themes 6+7 (vendor management, fragmented tools): Decision — "How Berkeley UAE eliminated manual dispatch entirely"
