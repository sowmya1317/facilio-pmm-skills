# /pmm-ai launch — AI Agent Launch Planner
**Invoke:** `/pmm-ai launch [mira | luca | fm-copilot | opsvision | atom-suite | custom feature]`

---

## Methodology

Same PMA launch tiering + Pragmatic Marketing + April Dunford methodology as `/pmm launch`, with three Atom-specific non-negotiables:

**1. Production First — Always**
Never announce an Atom agent before it is live in production with a named customer. "Coming soon" destroys credibility with AI-skeptical buyers.
- Before writing one word of launch copy: confirm the agent is live
- Confirm which customer is the proof anchor (Berkeley for Mira, Charter Hall for Luca)
- Confirm the specific metrics available to use (autonomous resolution rate, error detection, hours saved, timeline)

**2. "What It Kills" Leads Every Channel**
Every launch asset leads with what the FM team can stop doing — not what the agent can do.
- Mira: "Your team no longer needs to answer the 2am call."
- Luca: "Your finance team no longer reviews 2,117 invoices manually."
- OpsVision: "Your operations team no longer spends 3 days pulling the monthly report."
"What it kills" beats "introducing [agent name]" in every channel.

**3. Live vs. Roadmap in Every Launch Asset**
Every launch asset — email, LinkedIn, press release, sales one-liner — must include the production proof. No exceptions.
Format: "[Agent] is live at [Customer]. [Specific metric]. [Timeframe]."

---

## Execution Rules

- Tier classification: Mira + Luca = Tier 1 (standalone agents, major market shift) · Copilot + OpsVision = Tier 2 · Atom Suite = Tier 1
- Pre-launch problem amplification must run 2–4 weeks before announcement — the pain must be famous before the solution is announced
- Sales enablement must be complete before external launch — talk track, objection handler, demo script, FAQ all live before email goes out
- Launch email subject lines: never "Introducing [Agent Name]" — lead with the outcome or the proof stat
- LinkedIn launch post: hook line must work as a standalone scroll-stopper. Test it: would someone stop scrolling for this?

## Agent-Specific Launch Guidance

### Mira Launch (Tier 1)
**"What it kills":** "Your team no longer needs to answer the 2am call."
**Production proof:** Berkeley UAE — 276 calls · 175 SRs · ~80% autonomous · 30 days · 1 line 100% autonomous
**Pre-launch problem to amplify:** "FM helpdesks handle the same calls on repeat — at night, on weekends, during peak demand. Some go to voicemail. Some miss SLAs. All require a human."
**Email subject options:** "276 calls. Zero agents." / "Berkeley's helpdesk: 80% autonomous" / "The 2am call your team no longer takes"
**LinkedIn hook:** "Berkeley UAE's helpdesk line runs 100% autonomous. 276 calls in 30 days. Zero agents. Here's what that looks like on day 30."

### Luca Launch (Tier 1)
**"What it kills":** "Your finance team no longer reviews 2,117 invoices manually."
**Production proof:** Charter Hall — 2,117 invoices · 619 errors caught · detection 21%→39% · 70+ FM hours/month · 4 months
**Pre-launch problem to amplify:** "FM invoice reconciliation is the process no one automates — until a six-figure error slips through. Charter Hall's finance team was reviewing every invoice manually. 619 of them had errors."
**Email subject options:** "619 invoice errors. All caught before approval." / "Charter Hall: 70+ FM hours eliminated" / "The invoice error your team is about to approve"
**LinkedIn hook:** "Charter Hall processed 2,117 invoices in 4 months. Luca caught 619 errors before a single one reached approval. Their finance team now reviews flags, not invoices."

### FM Copilot Launch (Tier 2)
**"What it kills":** "Your team no longer switches between dashboards to answer one question."
**Proof:** Platform capability — 250–500 credits/session, web + mobile, NL queries + WO actions
**Email subject options:** "Ask it anything. Act on it immediately." / "Your CMMS, in plain English"

### OpsVision Launch (Tier 2)
**"What it kills":** "Your team no longer spends 3 days pulling the monthly client report."
**Proof:** Berkeley UAE — reporting cut from 5–7 days → instant one-click MMRs
**Email subject options:** "Monthly reports. One click." / "Berkeley: 5 days to instant"

### Atom Suite Launch (Tier 1 — Full Suite)
**"What it kills":** "Your team no longer manages software. Atom runs your FM operations."
**Production proof:** Berkeley (Mira) + Charter Hall (Luca) — both in production
**Email subject options:** "FM operations. Now autonomous." / "Berkeley + Charter Hall: what Atom looks like in production"
