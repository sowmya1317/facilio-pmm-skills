---

## Methodology

Build Atom messaging frameworks using the same three methodologies as `/pmm messaging` (StoryBrand + Challenger Nexus + Andy Raskin), with four Atom-specific rules:

**1. Lead with Execution, Not Intelligence**
The message is always the operational result, never the AI capability.
- RIGHT: "Mira handled 276 calls at Berkeley UAE in 30 days."
- WRONG: "Mira uses advanced NLP to understand service requests."
The proof of intelligence is in the execution outcome. Never explain the AI — show what it does.

**2. The Competitive Nexus**
"Legacy CMMS AI tells you what happened. Facilio Atom acts on it."
This must appear, adapted for context, in every messaging framework. It's the single sharpest reframe in the Atom story.

**3. Live vs. Roadmap Distinction**
Always separate what Atom does today vs. what competitors promise. Never put both in the same sentence. The live/roadmap distinction is what makes AI messaging credible to burned buyers.

**4. Human-in-the-Loop Framing**
Atom is not "replacing your team." It's "handling what your team doesn't have bandwidth for."
This is the trust bridge for skeptical buyers:
- "Mira handles the 2am calls. Your team handles the exceptions."
- "Luca catches the errors before they reach approval. Your finance team reviews the flags, not 2,117 invoices."

**Base methodology (inherited):**
- StoryBrand: Customer is hero. Facilio Atom is the guide. Three-layer problem (external/internal/philosophical).
- Challenger Nexus: One polarizing insight that makes the prospect feel the cost of inaction.
- Andy Raskin: "The world has changed" — establish the AI mandate landing on FM before positioning Atom.
- Voice-of-customer: Use words from Berkeley and Charter Hall proof, not marketing language.

---

## Execution Rules

- Never open with AI as a trend. Open with the operational result or the operational pain.
- Every framework must answer by the third value prop: "Is this live, or is this roadmap?" — answer definitively.
- Agent-level messaging must be specific: which agent, what it does, what it replaces, what Berkeley or Charter Hall metric proves it.
- Region nuance is mandatory — ME, UK, and ANZ have different entry points and proof anchors.
- The "Cost of Inaction" closer for AI messaging focuses on what each manual process costs: missed calls, undetected invoice errors, hours spent pulling reports.

## Segment-Specific Guidance

### FMSP / IFM Messaging (helpdesk-ai angle — lead with Mira)
**AI mandate:** FMSPs are being asked to do more with less. Headcount is frozen. Call volumes are rising. The AI mandate from leadership is: "Find a way to handle this without hiring."
**Nexus:** "Every FMSP thinks their helpdesk scaling problem is a staffing problem. Berkeley UAE solved it with zero new hires. Mira handled 276 calls in 30 days — 80% autonomously."
**Cost of inaction:** For every 100 calls/month your helpdesk misses or delays: calculate SLA breach rate × breach penalty × 12 months. That's the Mira ROI baseline.

### REIT / Owner-Operator Messaging (invoice-ai angle — lead with Luca)
**AI mandate:** Finance teams are under pressure to reduce processing overhead and improve accuracy. Invoice errors that slip through cost money and credibility with vendors.
**Nexus:** "Charter Hall's finance team was reviewing 2,117 invoices manually every 4 months. Luca caught 619 errors they were about to approve. That's not an efficiency story — that's a risk story."
**Cost of inaction:** Error rate × average invoice value × volume × 12 months = the annual risk exposure Luca eliminates.

### CDO / CAIO Messaging
**AI mandate:** Board has mandated AI. CDO/CAIO has been given ownership. FM is an obvious candidate — high volume, repetitive, measurable. But AI POCs in FM have a terrible track record.
**Nexus:** "The reason FM AI POCs fail isn't the technology. It's that the technology was built to advise your team, not replace a step in the process. Mira doesn't advise. It handles. Berkeley proved it in 30 days."
**Cost of inaction:** Every month the AI POC stays in evaluation is another month the board is asking why AI investment isn't delivering results.

### UK Region
**Entry point:** Compliance + reporting burden. UK FMSPs produce monthly client reports manually — often taking 5–7 days per report cycle.
**Lead agent:** OpsVision (reporting automation) or Luca (invoice accuracy for open book contracts)
**Proof anchor:** Berkeley UAE (reporting: 5–7 days → instant) · Verdantix 2025 Green Quadrant Leader credibility

### ME Region
**Entry point:** Voice + WhatsApp helpdesk volume. FM operations in ME run on WhatsApp. Mira speaks Arabic and handles multi-turn voice conversations.
**Lead agent:** Mira — the ME entry point for Atom
**Proof anchor:** Berkeley UAE — the primary ME reference, same region, same use case

### ANZ Region
**Entry point:** Invoice validation for REITs. Charter Hall is the primary ANZ AI reference.
**Lead agent:** Luca — leads all ANZ Atom conversations
**Proof anchor:** Charter Hall — 2,117 invoices, 619 errors, 70+ hours saved, 4 months
