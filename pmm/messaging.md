# /pmm messaging — Messaging Framework
**Invoke:** `/pmm messaging [fmsp | owner-operator | retail | ifm-uk | ifm-me | sla-compliance | vendor-management | client-reporting | budget-visibility]`

---

## Methodology

Build messaging frameworks using three frameworks combined:

**1. StoryBrand (Donald Miller)**
Customer is the hero. Facilio is the guide. Every message has three problem layers:
- External problem: The broken operational workflow (the visible, surface-level pain)
- Internal problem: The frustration, fear, or self-doubt it creates for the buyer
- Philosophical problem: The bigger truth — FM professionals deserve tools that work as hard as they do

**2. Challenger Sale — Nexus Insight**
One polarizing commercial insight that makes the prospect feel the cost of inaction — not the excitement of the solution. Must be:
- Specific to this segment (not generic AI trend language)
- Mildly uncomfortable to hear
- Something Facilio's proof directly disproves
Test: "Would a VP of FM feel mildly uncomfortable hearing this? Good."

**3. Andy Raskin — Strategic Narrative**
Lead with "the world has changed" — establish new stakes before positioning Facilio as the response.
Structure: Old game → New game → Why Facilio fits the new game.
The prospect must feel the shift before they're ready to hear about the solution.

**4. Voice-of-Customer (Copyhackers)**
Pull language from the proof quotes in the library. Prospects trust words that sound like them.
"In reality, there wasn't one tool" (Mark Hazelwood, Q3) is worth more than any copywriter's version.

---

## Execution Rules

- Never open with Facilio. Open with the prospect's world.
- The Nexus Insight is the hardest section to write — it must be specific and uncomfortable. Generic insights ("FM is changing") don't count.
- Headlines must be distinct angles — not the same message reworded five times.
- The "Cost of Inaction" closer is what sales uses to create urgency without discounting. Make it specific and costed.
- Proof points must match the segment — FMSP messaging uses Q3/Berkeley, owner-operator uses British Land/ICD Brookfield.

## Segment-Specific Guidance

### FMSP / IFM Messaging
**World has changed:** Clients now include CaFM requirements in RFPs — modern platforms with dashboards, mobile, IoT. FMs that can't show tech capability lose on paper before the presentation.
**Nexus insight:** "Every FMSP thinks their legacy CMMS is a cost center problem. It's actually a growth cap. Q3 didn't win 3x more contracts because they hired more people — they won because their platform let them say yes to things their competitors couldn't."
**Proof anchors:** Q3 (3x contracts, 2x revenue) · Berkeley UAE (80% AI resolution) · Quality Group (85% escalation reduction)
**ABM themes to layer in:** Pull from the 7 IFM ABM themes in skills.md — match to segment (National IFM → themes 1+2, Regional IFM → themes 3+7)

### Owner-Operator Messaging
**World has changed:** ESG mandates from investors, hybrid FM models creating visibility gaps, MRI/Yardi finance data sitting in a silo disconnected from operational reality.
**Nexus insight:** "When you outsource your FM operations, you don't just outsource the work. You outsource the data. And the data is what drives every CapEx decision, every lease renewal, every ESG report."
**Proof anchors:** British Land (35% energy, 4:1 ROI) · ICD Brookfield (5-month payback) · Investa (600 vendors streamlined) · Kingsmede (<24hr WO, 100% compliance)

### Use Case Messaging (SLA Compliance, Vendor Management, Client Reporting, Budget Visibility)
- SLA compliance: Lead with Quality Group (95–99% SLA adherence, 85% escalation reduction) — "SLA adherence isn't an ops metric. It's your contract renewal metric."
- Vendor management: Lead with IFM pain frequency data (89 mentions) — "Vendor management is the #1 pain FMSPs report. It's also the one most CaFMs handle the worst."
- Client reporting: Lead with Berkeley UAE (5–7 days → instant MMRs) — "Monthly reports shouldn't take a week to produce. They should take one click."
- Budget visibility: Lead with Charter Hall (619 invoice errors, 70+ FM hours saved) — "Every invoice your team reviews manually is one they could have caught automatically."
