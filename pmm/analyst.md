# /pmm analyst — Analyst Brief Builder
**Invoke:** `/pmm analyst [g2-review | gartner-brief | forrester-brief | verdantix-brief | rfi-response]`

---

## Methodology

Build analyst submissions using three frameworks:

**1. Gartner Magic Quadrant Criteria**
Two axes — every claim must map to one:
- Ability to Execute: Product viability, customer experience, market responsiveness, sales execution, overall viability
- Completeness of Vision: Market understanding, marketing strategy, sales strategy, offering/product strategy, innovation, geographic strategy

**2. Forrester Wave Methodology**
Three dimensions:
- Current Offering: Feature breadth, depth, integration ecosystem, AI maturity, mobile capability, UX
- Strategy: Roadmap credibility, vision, market approach, partner ecosystem
- Market Presence: Revenue context, customer count, growth trajectory, geographic reach

**3. Verdantix Green Quadrant (CMMS + CaFM)**
Facilio's primary analyst recognition. Evaluated on:
- Data quality and integration ecosystem
- Reporting depth and customization
- AI/analytics maturity (Facilio Atom is the evidence here)
- Mobile capability
- User experience
- ESG/sustainability reporting
Frame Facilio Atom explicitly against the AI/analytics criterion — this is where Facilio's 2025 Green Quadrant Leader status was earned.

**Analyst Credibility Rules:**
- No superlatives without evidence. "Industry-leading" without proof destroys credibility.
- No roadmap claims without dates. If it's not live, say "planned for [H/year]."
- No customer count without a verifiable reference. "150+ enterprise clients" is verifiable. "Hundreds of clients" is not.
- One fabricated claim destroys the brief. Analysts check.

---

## Execution Rules

- Lead with category framing — analysts don't care about features, they care about whether Facilio is defining a new category or competing in an existing one
- Every differentiation claim must include: what Facilio does, why a competitor can't credibly make the same claim, and the evidence
- Customer evidence must be verifiable — specific customer, specific metric, specific timeframe
- Tough questions section is mandatory — analysts ask hard questions. Pre-empt them.
- Never attack competitors directly in analyst briefs — reframe through category definition instead
