# /pmm positioning — Positioning Canvas
**Invoke:** `/pmm positioning [fmsp | owner-operator | retail | use-case-specific]`

---

## Methodology

Build positioning using three frameworks combined:

**1. April Dunford — "Obviously Awesome" (5-Component Positioning)**
Every positioning output must address all five components:
- Competitive alternatives: What the buyer would do if Facilio didn't exist (be honest — Maximo, FSI, spreadsheets, status quo)
- Unique attributes: What Facilio has that alternatives genuinely don't (not brand claims — provable differences)
- Value: What those attributes enable for the buyer (outcomes, not features)
- Target customers: Who cares most about that value (beachhead ICP — who feels the pain acutely enough to switch)
- Market frame of reference: The category context that makes Facilio's value instantly obvious

**2. "Only Facilio" Test**
Every positioning claim must pass: "Only Facilio [claim] because [unique attribute] proven by [evidence]."
If you can't complete that sentence with real proof — the claim is a brand assertion, not positioning.

**3. "So What?" Test**
Every statement must have a clear buyer implication. If the reaction is "so what?" — rewrite it.

**4. Category Design (Geoffrey Moore + Dunford)**
Don't position Facilio inside an existing category — define the category Facilio wants to own.
The positioning makes the problem famous before naming the solution.

---

## Execution Rules

- Never hedge. No "one of the leading" or "among the best." Make the claim or don't.
- Competitive alternatives must be honest — don't strawman Maximo or FSI. Acknowledge where they're strong.
- The "Only Facilio" statements are the sharpest output. Spend the most time here.
- UVP by buyer must be persona-specific — not the same sentence reworded five times.
- Messaging Dos and Don'ts section must always be included — it's what teams actually use.

## Segment-Specific Guidance

### FMSP Positioning
- Category to own: "The CaFM that wins RFPs and scales without headcount"
- Primary alternative: IBM Maximo (too legacy, SI-dependent) + spreadsheets/point solutions
- Sharpest "Only Facilio": Only Facilio deploys across a new client site in 3–4 months — proven by Q3 Services (all 300+ sites live without a single SI engagement)
- Frame of reference: Unified CaFM for all FM service lines and clients — not just a CMMS

### Owner-Operator Positioning
- Category to own: "The CaFM that gives you portfolio visibility regardless of your FM model"
- Primary alternative: MRI (finance-first, FM bolt-on) + outsourced CMMS (vendor-owned data)
- Sharpest "Only Facilio": Only Facilio connects finance and FM operations in one platform — proven by ICD Brookfield (5-month ROI) and British Land (4:1 ROI, 35% energy reduction)
- Frame of reference: Connected CaFM for owner-operators — not a property management tool

### Connected Buildings Positioning
- Category to own: "The only connected buildings platform that closes the loop from alarm to work order to resolution"
- Primary alternative: Siemens Navigator (energy only) · Clockworks/Atrius/Copper Tree (FDD only, no WO execution)
- Sharpest "Only Facilio": Only Facilio automatically converts a BMS alarm into an assigned, dispatched work order — no manual intervention, no tool-switching
- Frame of reference: Unified energy + FM operations platform — not an energy monitoring tool
